Privacy Policy — Prayer Room: Daily Plans & Peace

App: Prayer Room: Daily Plans & Peace (com.prayerroom.plans) · Last updated: 2026-09-02

This policy explains how Prayer Room: Daily Plans & Peace handles information. The app works offline and needs no account.

1. Data

We do not collect personal data ourselves. Progress and favorites are stored only on your device. The free version shows ads via Google AdMob, which may process limited technical identifiers to serve and measure ads.

2. Purchases

The optional Premium subscription is processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. You can manage or cancel it in the Google Play app.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Ad identifiers are processed by Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Children

This app is for a general audience. There is no chat or user-generated content.

5. Security & changes

No personal data is collected, so there is nothing identifying to protect. Ad and billing requests use HTTPS. We may update this policy; the date above shows the current version.
