Privacy Policy — Bible Jigsaw: Faith Puzzle Collection

App: Bible Jigsaw: Faith Puzzle Collection (com.faithpuzzlecollection) · Last updated: September 2, 2026

This policy explains how Bible Jigsaw: Faith Puzzle Collection ("the App", "we") handles information. The App works 100% offline and needs no account. We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone, contacts, precise location, or any data that personally identifies you or a child.

1. Data the App collects

We do not collect personal data ourselves. Your progress and best score are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, configured as child-directed (COPPA / Google Play Families Policy): ads are non-personalized, with no behavioral targeting or remarketing, limited to a “G” rating. Google may process limited technical identifiers needed to serve and measure non-personalized ads.

2. How the data is used

  • Show and measure ads;
  • Ad fraud prevention and safety;
  • App operation and performance.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. The identifiers above are processed by Google AdMob. See policies.google.com/privacy and how AdMob uses data.

4. Children

The App is directed to families and children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA. There is no sign-up, login, chat, social sharing, in-app purchases, or links to external content. Ads are non-personalized.

5. Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no identifying data to protect. Progress stays on the device only. Ad requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Your choices

You can uninstall the App at any time to erase local progress data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
