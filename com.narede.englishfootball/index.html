Privacy Policy — English Football Live

App: English Football Live (com.narede.englishfootball) · Developer: NaRede · Contact: placare.2026@gmail.com · Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the app handles information. The app shows live scores, tables and top scorers from public football competitions. You do not need an account to use it.

1. Data the app collects

We do not collect your name, email, phone number or precise location. The app uses these services:

  • Google AdMob shows ads and processes technical identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, to serve and measure them.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a device token to deliver the alerts you choose.
  • Firebase Analytics collects technical usage data to keep the app working and improve it.

Your favorite clubs and alert settings are stored on your device.

2. How the data is used

  • To show and measure ads;
  • To prevent fraud and keep ads secure;
  • To deliver alerts and keep the app working.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. The identifiers above are processed by Google. See policies.google.com/privacy and how AdMob uses data.

4. Audience

The app is for football fans in general and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Your choices

You can turn alerts off in the app, reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings, and uninstall the app at any time.

7. Independence

The app is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by the Premier League or any club. Club names and crests are used for information only.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.

9. Contact

Questions: placare.2026@gmail.com

Política de Privacidade — Futebol Inglês ao Vivo

O app mostra placares ao vivo, tabela e artilharia de competições públicas de futebol, sem necessidade de cadastro.

Não coletamos nome, e-mail, telefone ou localização precisa. Usamos o Google AdMob para exibir e medir anúncios, com identificadores técnicos como o ID de publicidade do Android; o Firebase Cloud Messaging para entregar os alertas que você escolher; e o Firebase Analytics para dados técnicos de uso. Seus times favoritos e configurações de alerta ficam no seu aparelho.

Não vendemos seus dados. Os identificadores são processados pelo Google, conforme policies.google.com/privacy. Os dados trafegam de forma criptografada (HTTPS). O app é para o público em geral e não é direcionado a crianças.
