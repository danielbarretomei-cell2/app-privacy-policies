Privacy Policy — Daily Grace

App: Daily Grace (com.dailygrace.devotional) · Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Policy explains how the app Daily Grace ("the App", "we") handles information. The App works simply and requires no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or any information that personally identifies you.

1. Data the App collects

We do not collect personal data directly. Local preferences (such as your notification time and your favorites) stay only on your device.

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. Depending on your Android settings and any applicable consent (GDPR), the ads may or may not be personalized. To serve and measure ads, Google may process limited technical identifiers and aggregated diagnostic data.

2. How this data is used

  • Serving and measuring ads;
  • Fraud prevention and ad safety;
  • App functionality, stability and performance.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. The identifiers above are processed by Google AdMob, under Google's privacy policy.

4. Retention and deletion

Preferences stay on the device and are removed when you uninstall the App. Data processed by Google follows Google's own retention periods.
